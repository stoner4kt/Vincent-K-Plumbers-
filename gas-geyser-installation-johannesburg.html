What you get

Gas geyser specialists for Johannesburg homes

Load-shedding does not have to mean cold showers. A gas geyser heats water on demand, so it keeps working whether the grid is up or down, and it typically cuts the geyser share of your electricity bill dramatically. We install continuous-flow gas water heaters in Klipfontein View, across Midrand and throughout Johannesburg, and we do the whole job: the gas line, the water connections, the weather protection and the compliance checks.

Every installation starts with an on-site assessment. We look at where the unit will live, how the gas line will run, what flueing the installation needs and how the hot water will reach your bathrooms and kitchen without long dead legs. You get a written quote before we start, and the price you agree is the price you pay.

Gas Geyser Installation services

What we do for Johannesburg homes and businesses

01

New gas geyser installations

Supply and fit 12L, 16L, 20L and larger constant-flow units for single bathrooms, family homes and multi-outlet properties.

02

Electric-to-gas conversions

Retire an ageing 150L electric geyser in favour of an on-demand gas unit, including the changeover of water pipework and the removal of the old cylinder.

03

Outdoor weather boxes and housings

Purpose-built enclosures that keep the unit protected from rain and sun while staying ventilated and serviceable.

04

Flues, cowls and ventilation

Correct flue routing and termination so exhaust gases leave the building safely and the installation passes inspection.

05

Gas line installation

Copper or approved gas piping from the cylinder to the unit, with regulators, isolators and pressure testing.

06

Servicing and repairs

Descaling, burner cleaning, ignition faults, water-temperature fluctuations and leaking connections.
