What you get

Paving built to stay level

Most paving failures we are called to fix were never the bricks — they were the base. Insufficient excavation, no compaction, no edge restraint and no fall away from the house are what cause sinking, rocking and puddles a season later. We strip and prepare properly, and we set the falls so water leaves the property instead of pooling against your walls.

We lay block paving, clay brick paving and pavers around pools, driveways, patios, walkways and courtyards. Edging is fixed with concrete or appropriate restraint, joints are kiln-dried sand filled, and the finished surface is compacted and cleaned down. If drainage or irrigation needs to go in first, we coordinate it so nothing has to be dug up twice.
